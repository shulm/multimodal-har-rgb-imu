# Data and artifact boundaries

Historical experiments used UTD-MHAD RGB and inertial recordings. Consult the
[official dataset page](https://personal.utdallas.edu/~kehtar/UTD-MHAD.html) for
dataset access, terms and citation. Availability of a download is not a grant to
mirror data. This repository includes no participant media, raw sensor recordings,
derived embeddings, per-record exports, replay arrays, reference banks or weights.
Aggregate metrics are labeled historical development evidence.

Reference: Chen Chen, Roozbeh Jafari and Nasser Kehtarnavaz, “UTD-MHAD: A Multimodal
Dataset for Human Action Recognition Utilizing a Depth Camera and a Wearable
Inertial Sensor,” IEEE International Conference on Image Processing, 2015.
Synthetic examples are generated from zeros or artificial numeric sequences and
do not depict participants. Users must obtain permission for any local real inputs.
